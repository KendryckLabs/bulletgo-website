BulletGo

Smart Delivery Offer Insights

BulletGo helps delivery drivers evaluate supported offers using custom stores, filters, match alerts, activity history and statistics.

Features

Custom Filters

Configure the offer criteria that matter to you.

My Stores

Choose the stores you want your filters to consider.

Match Alerts

Receive alerts when supported offers match your preferences.

Activity

Review your recent matching offers.

Statistics

See useful insights calculated from your local match history.

Privacy-first

BulletGo v1 processes supported offer information locally on your device. The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or BulletGo-operated cloud processing of offer information.
